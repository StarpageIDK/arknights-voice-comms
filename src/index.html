How to play

Identify the operator in five attempts. The first line is always “Arknights”. Each incorrect guess or skip unlocks another voice line. Replay any unlocked attempt. Voice lines 2–5 change each round, with longer lines later and a trust conversation at the end.

Hints reveal rarity, class, then race and faction. Each language has its own statistics. Switching language starts a new round without counting the unfinished round. Click the next locked attempt to skip. Click the playing attempt to pause.
